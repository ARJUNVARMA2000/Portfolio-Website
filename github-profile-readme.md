## About Me

Currently pursuing my **Master's in Data Science at Columbia University**, I bring 3+ years of experience from ZS Associates where I worked with Fortune 500 healthcare clients on ML platforms, predictive analytics, and LLM-powered solutions.

I specialized in transforming complex data into actionable insights using Python, SQL, PySpark, and modern ML frameworks. My work spanned from building organization-wide analytics platforms serving 1,000+ users to developing early cancer detection models across 250M+ patient records.

At Columbia, I serve as a **Teaching Assistant** for Business Analytics II: Foundations of AI and Hollywood and Big Data at Columbia Business School, helping students bridge the gap between theoretical concepts and practical applications.
